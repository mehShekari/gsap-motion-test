Test C — Ambiguous Real-World Motion

Prompt:

Make this landing page feel alive and premium.

Improve the motion and interaction design of the existing page without changing its core visual identity or content.

The experience should guide the user's attention naturally from the hero toward the primary CTA and then through the feature content.

Motion should establish hierarchy, continuity, and a sense of progression rather than making individual elements animate independently.

Some interactions should respond to the user's input where appropriate, but avoid decorative motion that does not improve the experience.

The page must remain comfortable to use on mobile, desktop, keyboard navigation, and for users who prefer reduced motion.

Use GSAP for the motion implementation.

You decide what should move, when it should move, how it should move, and which interaction patterns are appropriate.

Before finishing:

Inspect the existing page and identify the most important opportunities for motion.
Implement the motion system.
Review the result at desktop and mobile sizes.
Remove or refine any motion that feels unnecessary, distracting, or disconnected.
Verify lifecycle, responsiveness, accessibility, and performance.

Do not redesign the page.

Do not add animation merely to demonstrate GSAP features.

Prioritize the quality of the overall experience over the number of animated elements.
